Maintenance Notices

Seasonal clean-up and landscaping schedules are posted here to support ongoing grounds maintenance.

Spring Cleanup (April 1–20)

Winter decorations and wreaths should be removed prior to April 1. Items remaining during this period may be removed as part of seasonal maintenance.

Fall Cleanup (November 1–30)

Seasonal decorations and planting containers should be removed prior to November 1 to assist with leaf removal and winter preparation.
