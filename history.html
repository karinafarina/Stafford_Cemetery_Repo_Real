Our History

Stafford Springs Cemetery has served our community for over a century, preserving the legacy of those who helped shape the area's early development. As a nonprofit 501(c)(13) corporation, we remain committed to honoring the memory of all those who rest within our grounds.

Early Years

Founded in the 19th century, Stafford Springs Cemetery has been a cornerstone of our community's heritage. The grounds were carefully established to provide a peaceful, dignified resting place for families and their loved ones.

The Harwood Chapel

In 1929, the Harwood Chapel was constructed, creating a quiet and intimate space for memorial services and celebrations of life. This architectural landmark remains a cherished part of our cemetery's identity and continues to serve families during their time of need.

Ongoing Stewardship

Throughout the decades, Stafford Springs Cemetery has maintained its commitment to preservation, accessibility, and dignity. Our nonprofit governance ensures transparent stewardship, strong ethical standards, and long-term sustainability for generations to come.
