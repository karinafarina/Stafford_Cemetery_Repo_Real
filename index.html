About Stafford Springs Cemetery

About

Stafford Springs Cemetery preserves our community's heritage while providing compassionate, professional care. As a nonprofit 501(c)(13), we uphold strong governance, ethical standards, and transparent stewardship.

Our focus is long-term preservation, accessibility, and dignity for every family we serve.
