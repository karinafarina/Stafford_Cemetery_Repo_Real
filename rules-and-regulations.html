Rules and Regulations

Stafford Springs Cemetery operates under a comprehensive set of guidelines to maintain the grounds, preserve the dignity of the cemetery, and ensure a peaceful environment for families and visitors.

Lot and Memorial Guidelines

All lot owners are responsible for maintaining their plots in good condition. Markers and monuments must be installed according to cemetery specifications. We prohibit artificial flowers, standing arrangements, and items that may interfere with groundskeeping operations. Plantings and decorations must be approved by cemetery staff.

Visitation and Conduct

The cemetery is open during designated hours for quiet reflection and visitation. We ask all visitors to conduct themselves respectfully. Loud noise, photography without permission, and commercial activities are not permitted. Please remain on designated pathways and dispose of trash properly.

Services and Ceremonies

All funeral services, interments, and memorial events must be scheduled in advance through the cemetery office. The Harwood Chapel is available for services with appropriate notice. Fees apply for all services; contact our office for current pricing.

Maintenance and Care

Stafford Springs Cemetery staff perform regular groundskeeping and maintenance. Lot owners should contact the office before placing any items on graves. The cemetery reserves the right to remove items that do not comply with our regulations.

Perpetual Care

As a nonprofit cemetery corporation, we are committed to perpetual care of all lots. Lot owners are encouraged to establish endowments to support long-term preservation and maintenance of their family plots.
